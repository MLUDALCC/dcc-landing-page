Thank You

Thank you for your gift

Because of you, young singers across North Texas will keep learning, performing, and growing together. Your receipt is on its way to the email address you used at checkout.

On behalf of every chorister, thank you. Your generosity does more than fill a spot on the risers. It gives a child the discipline, friendships, and joy of making music together, and it gives our community something beautiful to hear.

We’re so grateful you’re part of the DCC family.

— The Board of Trustees, Dallas Children’s Chorus
Where Your Gift Goes

Your gift fills the gap

80–85%

of the cost is covered by gifts, grants, sponsors, and ticket sales

Tuition covers only about 15–20% of what it costs to train and present the DCC’s choristers, so the remaining 80–85% depends on supporters like you, along with foundations, corporate partners, and ticket sales.

Your gift helps make sure a chorister’s place in the chorus is never decided by a family’s ability to pay full tuition.

The exact share varies from year to year with the number of choristers and the amount of tuition assistance provided.

Your Receipt

Keep this for your records

A receipt is on its way to your email. The Dallas Children’s Chorus is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization (EIN 41-5362609), and your gift is tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

If you don’t see the email within a few minutes, please check your spam folder or write to us at chorus@dalcc.org.
