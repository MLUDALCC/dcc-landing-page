Official Children’s Chorus of the Dallas Symphony Orchestra

Where young voices become extraordinary.

The Dallas Children’s Chorus trains and inspires young singers through rigorous musicianship and performances with a world-class symphony orchestra. As the new home of the Dallas Symphony Children’s Chorus beginning with the 2027/28 season, the DCC continues as the official children’s chorus of the Dallas Symphony Orchestra — building on a tradition that includes the more than four decades of service by the Dallas Symphony Chorus.

Mission

To inspire generations of young singers through exceptional musical training and world-class orchestral collaborations, setting the standard for children’s choral artistry.

A Welcome From Our Artistic Director

Welcome to the Dallas Children’s Chorus

If you love to sing and dream of collaborating with a world-class orchestra, this is the place for you. At the DCC, our goal is to instill a love for music, build confidence, foster teamwork through hands-on experience, and enhance music reading skills and singing technique. With a vision to create harmony with other passionate and talented singers at a young age, DCC’s singers will find life-long joy in achieving excellence and becoming compassionate, empathetic individuals — all while having fun.

Ellie Lin
Artistic Director

Our Connection to the Dallas Symphony Orchestra

As the official children’s chorus of the Dallas Symphony Orchestra, DCC choristers have the extraordinary opportunity to perform with one of the nation’s leading orchestras. These experiences challenge young singers to meet the high standards of a world-class orchestra while developing the musicianship, discipline, confidence, and artistry that extend far beyond the concert hall.
