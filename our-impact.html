About the DCC

Our Impact

The DCC brings young singers from across North Texas together to train and perform at the highest level — and works to keep that opportunity open to every child who wants it.

Access

Keeping music education within reach

We keep tuition low on purpose, to broaden the circle of children who can sing with us. Tuition covers only about 15%–20% of what it costs to train and present our choristers, so the remaining 80%–85% depends on gifts, grants, sponsors, and ticket sales. That support also makes need-based tuition assistance possible, so that cost is less of a barrier to joining.

The Pathway

A place to grow, from fourth grade through high school

Every chorister receives training built for their voice, age, and experience. Choristers typically begin in Training Choir, move to Symphonic Voices (a treble choir for unchanged voices), and then join Mixed Ensemble once their voices have changed.

  1. 1

    Training Choir

    Grades 4–6

    Teaches the fundamentals of singing and music learning — the entry point for young choristers.

  2. 2

    Symphonic Voices

    Grades 6–8 · Treble choir

    Our premier treble ensemble, which performs regularly with the Dallas Symphony Orchestra.

  3. 3

    Mixed Ensemble

    Grades 9–12 · SATB

    Our SATB choir for experienced youths, singing four-part harmony and some of the chorus’s most demanding repertoire.

Artistic Excellence

Three major collaborations with the DSO in four seasons

As the official children’s chorus of the Dallas Symphony Orchestra, our choristers have sung some of the most demanding works in the choral-orchestral repertoire — in addition to performing in the DSO’s Christmas Pops concerts every season.
