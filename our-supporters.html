With Gratitude

Our Supporters

Every gift helps young singers in North Texas train and perform at the highest level. We are deeply grateful to the individuals, families, foundations, and businesses who make the Dallas Children’s Chorus possible.

Those Who Stepped Forward

Founding Supporters

Gifts and in-kind support received through February 28, 2027

When the Dallas Symphony Children’s Chorus becomes the independent Dallas Children’s Chorus, it will carry with it the trust of those who placed their confidence in the DCC and its mission from the very start. Founding Supporters are those whose gifts and in-kind support were received through February 28, 2027, as we prepare to launch our first independent season. By giving, they affirmed a belief we share: that a children’s chorus performing alongside a world-class orchestra is rare, and worth preserving and nurturing. Their generosity will always be part of our story.

    Our Founding Supporters will be recognized here.

    With thanks to our founding corporate partners
    2027/28 Season

    Inaugural Season Supporters

    These supporters help sustain our first full season as the Dallas Children’s Chorus. Inaugural Season Supporters are those whose gifts and in-kind support were received from March 1, 2027 through the end of the 2027/28 season.

      A Note on Recognition

      Listed by Your Choice

      We list supporters exactly as they ask to be named, never by gift amount. Donors may choose to have one name appear or both names of a couple, in the order they prefer. Donors who would rather not be named can check the box on our giving form to keep their name out of public recognition, or contact us at any time to change how they are listed.
