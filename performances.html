DCC choristers take the stage throughout the year, from the Morton H. Meyerson Symphony Center to community venues across North Texas. The performances below are presented by the Dallas Symphony Children’s Chorus (DSCC) for the 2026/27 season, ahead of its transition to the Dallas Children’s Chorus in 2027/28.
