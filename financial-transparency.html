About the DCC

Financial Transparency

The families, donors, and funders who support the Dallas Children’s Chorus should be able to see how we are organized and funded. Here is the essential information in one place.

At a Glance

How We Are Funded

Tuition covers only part of the cost

Tuition covers only about 15–20% of what it costs to train and present our choristers. The remaining 80–85% comes from gifts, grants, sponsors, and ticket sales. The exact share varies from year to year with the number of choristers and the amount of tuition assistance provided.

Governance

Overseen by a volunteer board

The Dallas Children’s Chorus is governed by a volunteer Board of Trustees, entrusted with the organization’s financial stewardship and strategic direction.

Available on request

Copies of our IRS determination letter and our annual IRS filings are available on request. Please email chorus@dalcc.org and we will be glad to send them.
