Ways to Give

There are several ways to make a gift to the DCC — choose whichever method works best for you and your financial goals.

Giving Options

Explore your options below

Give quickly and securely by credit or debit card — click below to open our secure giving form.

To make a gift by wire transfer, please contact us at chorus@dalcc.org and our team will provide the necessary account and routing details.
We welcome gifts recommended through your donor advised fund. Please direct your DAF sponsor to Dallas Childrens Chorus Inc (EIN 41-5362609).

Checks should be mailed to:

Dallas Children’s Chorus
11700 Preston Rd, Ste 660–644
Dallas, TX 75230
If you are 70½ or older, you may be able to make a tax-free gift directly from your IRA. Please direct your IRA custodian (e.g., Fidelity, Merrill Lynch, Schwab, Vanguard, etc.) to send the distribution via check in the amount you desire (up to the allowable limit) payable to Dallas Children’s Chorus, Inc.

Checks should be mailed to:

Dallas Children’s Chorus
11700 Preston Rd, Ste 660–644
Dallas, TX 75230

We recommend consulting your tax advisor to confirm your eligibility and the tax benefits of a qualified charitable distribution.
To give via check, please ensure that all checks are made payable to Dallas Children’s Chorus, Inc. List the intended purpose in the memo section or accompanying correspondence. Checks should be mailed to:

Dallas Children’s Chorus
11700 Preston Rd, Ste 660–644
Dallas, TX 75230
Gifts of appreciated securities or stock held by your bank or brokerage firm, or by you, offer tax advantages for donors and are easy to make. Your brokerage firm may transfer gifts of stock directly into the DCC’s brokerage account. To make a gift of appreciated securities, please contact us first at chorus@dalcc.org — our team will provide the brokerage details needed to complete the transfer.
