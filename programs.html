Sing With Us

Programs

The Dallas Children’s Chorus offers a multi-tiered choral curriculum for children and youth in grades 4–12, with an emphasis on artistic excellence and representation of Dallas’s diverse communities. Every chorister progresses through training built specifically for their voice, age, and experience.

Training Choir

Grades 4–8 · Unchanged Voices

Training Choir teaches the fundamentals of singing and music learning — the entry point for young choristers building the foundation they’ll carry through each level that follows — and is led by Director Brittany Haywood and Artistic Director Ellie Lin. It is open to students with unchanged treble voices entering grades 4–8 in the autumn.

Director
Brittany Haywood
Artistic Director
Ellie Lin
Rehearsal
Mondays, 6:00–7:00 PM
Location
Lovers Lane United Methodist Church
Audition
No prepared song required — simple vocalizations and echo singing to assess vocal range and pitch-matching
Tuition
$450/season

Schedule and tuition reflect the current DSCC season and will be confirmed as the DCC takes over program operations for 2027/28.

Symphonic Voices

Grades 4–8 · Treble Choir · Unchanged Voices

Symphonic Voices is the Dallas Children’s Chorus’s premier treble ensemble — the choir that performs regularly with the Dallas Symphony Orchestra — and is led by Artistic Director Ellie Lin and Assistant Director Brittany Haywood. It is open to students with unchanged treble voices entering grades 4–8 in the autumn.

Artistic Director
Ellie Lin
Assistant Director
Brittany Haywood
Rehearsal
Mondays, 7:00–8:45 PM
Saturdays, 2:00–4:00 PM (occasional)
Location
Lovers Lane United Methodist Church
Audition
No prepared song required — vocalization and echo singing to assess vocal range and pitch-matching
Tuition
$500/season

Schedule and tuition reflect the current DSCC season and will be confirmed as the DCC takes over program operations for 2027/28.

Mixed Ensemble

Grades 9–12 · SATB

Mixed Ensemble is the Dallas Children’s Chorus’s high-school SATB (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass) choir, giving experienced singers the chance to grow into four-part harmony and take on some of the chorus’s most demanding repertoire, and is led by Director Victor Johnson and Assistant Director Cory Curvin. It is open to students entering grades 9–12 in the autumn, as well as those in grade 8 with changing or changed voices.

Director
Victor Johnson
Assistant Director
Cory Curvin
Rehearsal
Mondays, 7:00–9:00 PM
Location
Lovers Lane United Methodist Church
Audition
Prepared song required, preferably from the UIL Prescribed Music List, Class 1 or 2
Tuition
$600/season

Schedule and tuition reflect the current DSCC season and will be confirmed as the DCC takes over program operations for 2027/28.

The Dallas Children’s Chorus is committed to fostering a culture of diversity and inclusion.

Need-based financial assistance is available. A tuition assistance application is included in the welcome packet sent at the beginning of each concert season — please reach out to our team at chorus@dalcc.org to learn more.
