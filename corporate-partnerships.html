Corporate Partnerships

Partner With Us

Corporate partners help the Dallas Children’s Chorus provide music training and performance opportunities for young singers across North Texas.

Why Partner

Supporting the Chorus

The Dallas Children’s Chorus is the official children’s chorus of the Dallas Symphony Orchestra. Our choristers receive rigorous musical training and perform with the orchestra and in concerts across the region. Corporate partners may support a season, a single performance, or programs such as tuition assistance.

We do not offer fixed packages. Each partnership is arranged to suit both the company and the chorus.

Ways to Partner

Forms of Support

  • Financial Support

    Support the chorus across a season, underwrite a particular concert, or help fund training and tuition assistance. We will discuss the option that best fits your goals.

  • In-Kind Support

    Contribute goods or services, such as printing, catering, event space, or professional expertise.

Many employers match charitable gifts made by their employees. We are glad to provide any information your matching-gift program requires.

Recognition

Partner Recognition

Recognition is agreed with each partner at the outset. Depending on the nature of the support, it may include:

  • Your logo on our Our Supporters page
  • Your name or logo in concert programs
  • Acknowledgment in our announcements and on social media
  • Tickets for employees and guests or, where our schedule allows, a performance for your team
With thanks to our founding corporate partners
Getting Started

Starting a Partnership

  1. Initial conversation. Tell us about your company and your interests.
  2. Agree on the arrangement. We will discuss the options and confirm the support and recognition.
  3. Ongoing connection. We will keep you informed about the chorus’s concerts and activities.

The Dallas Children’s Chorus is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 41-5362609). Gifts may be tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law; please consult your tax advisor.

Next Steps

Discuss a partnership.

To discuss a partnership, write to chorus@dalcc.org or use our contact page. A member of our team will respond.
