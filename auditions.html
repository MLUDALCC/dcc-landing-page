Auditions

Auditions for the Dallas Children’s Chorus’s 2027/28 season will be held in April and May 2027. Requirements vary by grade level — details below.

Audition dates and times will be announced in late winter or early spring 2027. Please check back here for updates, or reach out to our team with any questions.

Interested in auditioning for 2027/28? Let us know, and we’ll send audition dates and details as soon as they’re available.

Requirements

Audition Requirements

Grades 4–8

Students in grades 4–8 do not need to prepare a song. They will be asked to do some simple vocalizations and echo singing to help determine vocal range and ability to match pitch.

Grades 9–12

Students in grades 9–12 will need to prepare a song, preferably one selected from the UIL Prescribed Music List, Class 1 or 2.
