Support the DCC

An Investment in Young Musicians

Supporting the DCC is a unique opportunity to invest in the musical and personal development of young singers, while advancing the cultural life of our community.

Hear the DCC

See the difference your support makes

Your generosity helps sustain and grow a premier children’s chorus, providing transformative opportunities for young musicians that would otherwise be out of reach. Tuition covers only about 15–20% of what it costs to train and present our choristers (the exact share varies from year to year), so your support also helps fund need-based tuition assistance — making sure a chorister’s place here is never decided by a family’s ability to pay full tuition.

Below, hear the combined choruses of the Dallas Symphony Children’s Chorus performing “United in Song” by Victor C. Johnson — a piece that captures exactly what your support makes possible: young voices coming together to create something extraordinary. Thank you for helping make moments like this possible.

Why I Give

Hear from the donors who make it possible

Every gift comes from a personal reason. Our donors are parents, trustees, and friends of the chorus who believe in music’s power to shape young lives. Here’s why they give.

“The Dallas Children’s Chorus is a life-changing program, not only for my daughter but for all the children involved. It has brought children together from all over the DFW Metroplex, from as far north as Leonard to as far south as Kemp, and all areas in between. Not only are these children learning music, but they are also creating bonds and friendships through their shared love of music that will help shape the adults they become. I cannot think of a better children’s arts program to contribute to.”

— Robin Marin, DCC Parent & Volunteer

“I was able to play in my city’s youth orchestra because of donors who generously paid it forward. Given its history and affiliation with the DSO, the Dallas Children’s Chorus is special — a unique asset for children’s performing arts across North Texas, and one worth nurturing and protecting. As a kid, I benefited from the generosity of others. It’s now my turn to pay it forward.”

— Michael Lu, DCC Parent & Trustee

“There’s a magic about making music, and the finest way to make children sensible of that magic is to have them make the music themselves. As the parent of three children who sing in the Children’s Chorus, I have had the joy of watching that magic become real. When children raise their voices in song, they discover not only the beauty of music, but also the power of listening, working together, and being part of something greater than themselves.”

— Kate Lindsey, DCC Parent & Trustee
